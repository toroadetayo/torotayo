Info

Adetoro is a sustainability and impact strategist with a foundation in chemical engineering.

I translate technical complexity into actionable insight, helping organizations turn environmental and social challenges into strategies that hold up under pressure.

I co-founded Susbridge and co-publish Susinsight, building infrastructure that makes sustainability knowledge more accessible across Africa. Through Susbridge's research, data, and consulting services, I support institutions and businesses in navigating regulatory landscapes, assessing impact, and aligning operations with long-term goals.

My background spans industrial quality systems, water treatment research, and regulatory compliance. I've designed wastewater solutions using predictive modeling and lifecycle assessment, supported SMEs through climate transitions, advised ed-tech startups on retention strategies, and strengthened manufacturing standards across Nigeria's food and beverage sectors.

I hold a Master's with Distinction in Sustainable Chemical Engineering from the University of Bath and a First Class Honours degree in Chemical Engineering from Covenant University.

What Drives This Work

What drives my work is a conviction that sustainability must be practical, not performative. I'm drawn to partnerships that demand both rigor and honesty, collaborations where context shapes the solution, and where research, strategy, and storytelling come together to create lasting impact.

If you need strategic insight grounded in systems thinking, research that holds weight, or a partner who connects technical depth with real-world application, Let's connect.

Beyond the Work

I'm a Christian, wife to Tomi, and a lifelong learner. I spent much of my life in Northern Nigeria and now live on the outskirts of Lagos.

My journey has been profoundly shaped by faith, family, and an enduring commitment to growth. My interests bridge the sciences, arts, fitness, and technology, reflecting a belief that Sustainability, like life, thrives on balance and curiosity.
