Life

I grew up in Northern Nigeria and have since lived in Lagos and Bath. Sydney is home now. Each place has changed the questions I ask and the people I feel connected to.

I’m a Christian, a wife, and a lifelong learner. The sciences, arts, fitness and technology all have a place in my world. So does the quieter work of building a life with the people I love.

Career

I studied Chemical Engineering at Covenant University and Sustainable Chemical Engineering at the University of Bath. Since then, my work has taken me through research, sustainability strategy, data, mentoring and communication.

I’m interested in what happens when technical work becomes useful to people making real decisions. That interest has led me into energy, materials, climate and public knowledge projects.

Research

I’m a doctoral researcher at Western Sydney University, studying how high-tech greenhouses can use energy, water and nutrients more efficiently. The work looks at how those needs change with the season, the crop and the way a greenhouse is run.

I use process modelling, economic analysis and partial life cycle assessment to compare options and help growers understand the trade-offs.

Hawkesbury Institute for the Environment, Western Sydney University
